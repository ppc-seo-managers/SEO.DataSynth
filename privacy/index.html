Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

DataSynth ("DataSynth", "we", "us") provides reporting and dashboard tools that agencies use to manage their clients' marketing accounts. This policy explains what data DataSynth accesses when an authorized user connects a third-party platform account (such as Pinterest, Google, Meta or other advertising and analytics services), how that data is used, and the choices available to you.

Who uses DataSynth

DataSynth is used by authorized agency staff only. It is not offered to the general public, and it does not collect data from consumers who browse or use the connected platforms.

Data we access

When an authorized user signs in to a connected platform through that platform's official authorization (OAuth) flow, DataSynth receives an access token limited to the permissions the user approves. Depending on the platform and the permissions granted, DataSynth may read:

  • Basic account information of the signed-in user (account name, username and account ID).
  • Advertising account details: ad account names, IDs, currency, time zone and the user's role.
  • Campaign, ad group and ad metadata: names, IDs, status and budgets.
  • Aggregated performance metrics: spend, impressions, clicks, conversions and similar reporting data, by day.
  • Website analytics and search performance data for properties the user manages.

DataSynth does not read Pins, boards, direct messages, followers or other personal content of platform users, and does not collect personal data about the people who see or click on ads.

How we use data

  • To show performance and spend reporting for accounts the agency manages.
  • To track advertising spend against client budgets and alert the agency team to pacing issues.
  • To verify that a connection is working (for example, a periodic test call).

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, use it to build user profiles, or share it with data brokers. We do not use data obtained from connected platforms to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Google API Services

DataSynth's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Pinterest

Data received from the Pinterest API is used only to provide reporting on advertising accounts the signed-in user manages, in line with the Pinterest Developer Guidelines.

Sharing

Data is visible only to authorized staff of the agency that connected the account. We share data with service providers that host and operate DataSynth (cloud database and hosting providers) solely to run the service, and when required by law. We do not otherwise disclose data to third parties.

Storage and security

  • Access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest in a secrets vault and are never sent to a web browser.
  • Data is stored in access-controlled databases; every user must sign in, and access is limited by role.
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Retention and deletion

Reporting data is kept for as long as the agency manages the account and needs it for reporting. When a connection is removed, its tokens are deleted immediately. You can revoke DataSynth's access at any time from the connected platform's settings (for Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps). To request deletion of data associated with an account, contact us at the address below; we respond within 30 days.

Children

DataSynth is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes
